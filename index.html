What changed from v1 (35 mappings)

  • Role badge rename: Anchor → Medical Anchor (MBBS/MD) or Therapeutic Anchor (BAMS/BHMS). Driver → Active Treatment Lead. Accel → Core Accelerator. Comp → Compliance Anchor. All now match Expert Carrd exactly.
  • BHMS integrated as alternative Therapeutic Anchor in integrative/chronic clusters (gut, chronic, hormonal) wherever BAMS appears — patient or team may indicate preference.
  • Cluster G added — Immunity & Prevention (3 new mappings): covers patients who have no acute complaint but are proactively seeking immune support, pollution defence, or seasonal wellness.
  • Cluster H added — Fertility & Conception (4 new mappings): separates fertility from the hormonal cluster, as fertility cases have a distinct team structure including a dedicated women's health specialist.
  • Essential plan now always has a medical or therapeutic anchor — resolved the gap where Essential had no clinical presence.
  • Fallback mapping updated: now routes to a BAMS/BHMS Therapeutic Anchor + Dietician on Essential, not just MBBS + Dietician — more economical for the lowest plan floor.
  • Dynamic multi-phase flag added to all hormonal and fertility mappings above Essential tier.
